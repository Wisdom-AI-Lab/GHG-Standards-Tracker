# Selected real requirements: scope and evidence

The Atlas presentation uses fictional client facts with four selected real reporting screens. The underlying official sources were read on 27 August 2026. They are **source checked, human review pending**. No actual client compliance determination is made.

## Included routes

| Screen | What is evaluated | Main boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AU-SIZE | Australian ordinary-company scope, Chapter 2M prerequisite and the applicable two-of-three corporate-size test | Not NGER, asset-owner routes or a full relief assessment |
| SGX-S12 | Explicit Mainboard coverage and the selected Scope 1/2 commencement | Not every listing regime or a full sustainability-report assessment |
| SGX-STI-CLIMATE | Mainboard coverage, historical STI membership and the corresponding climate-disclosure commencement | Only the selected STI route; not non-STI market-capitalisation routes |
| SGX-STI-S3 | The historical STI route's separate Scope 3 commencement | Other routes, voluntary reporting and assurance are not determined |

Exact conditions, dates and official citations are in [requirements.mjs](../data/requirements.mjs); research locators and source limitations are recorded in [SOURCE_LOG.md](SOURCE_LOG.md). The fixture does not reproduce standards text.

## Client facts and dates

Every Atlas ownership, control, financial, Mainboard, historical index and relief input is fictional. The UK entity's hypothetical Mainboard status demonstrates why incorporation country alone is insufficient. Its historical STI membership is deliberately null; unknown is not false.

The organisation date controls transaction overlays. The financial-year start selects the reporting phase and the exact financial-input record. A missing financial period stays unknown; amounts are not copied from another year. The selectable periods contain explicit sample figures, not real-company forecasts. AU amounts represent supplied consolidated AUD totals and employee counts; they are not calculated from the GHG boundary method or ownership allocation.

The screen is a planning projection. It does not claim the relevant year has ended, that actual year-end evidence exists, or that future legal changes have been predicted. It uses the checked rule pack to compare reporting periods; it is not a reconstruction of which source text was known on a historical date.

## Result meanings

- **Screen matched:** the fictional inputs satisfy the selected route and the fixture's no-relief/no-waiver assumption. This is not full compliance, an assurance opinion or human approval.
- **Selected route not matched:** one or more required conditions fail. Other reporting routes can still apply.
- **Requires more facts:** a required input is unknown, or a relief/waiver position needs assessment. The row names missing evidence.
- **Before selected route starts:** the selected financial year precedes this route's commencement. This is not a conclusion about earlier or alternative regimes.
- **Coverage not researched:** the instrument or entity type is outside the implemented pack. UK domestic coverage remains visible as a gap.

Group inclusion is separate from every local screen. Acquisition/divestment changes the group-view label and metrics; local conditions are still evaluated. A change table counts status or membership differences, not all possible changes in legal text or financial data.

## Excluded from this build

Full Australian NGER/asset-owner routes; special investment/superannuation structures; statutory consolidation and aggregation; detailed reporting substitutions and relief; Singapore non-STI phased routes, new-listing analysis, Catalist and individual waivers; UK domestic rules; filing deadlines; detailed disclosure content; assurance phasing; and comprehensive cross-border analysis.

The graph root and facility are not independently assessed. No conclusion about every Atlas entity or every relevant obligation should be inferred. The optional PepsiCo public extract never runs these screens because its supporting entity facts have not been established.

## Extension and review gate

Before adding another route, record its authoritative source, locator, applicable version, effective/reporting periods, scope, relief and required fact units. Test exact boundaries, unknown inputs, alternate routes and source exclusions. Obtain human regulatory review before client reliance. Recheck sources before a later presentation; no monitoring or automatic legal refresh is configured.
